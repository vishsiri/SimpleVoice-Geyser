## Join MineDream Voice Chat

### Other Guides
- Flags: [Chrome Flags]({% project_link joining/chrome-flags %})

1. Join the MineDream Minecraft server with the account linked to your Webshop account.
2. Open `https://voicechat.minedream.city` and sign in through the MineDream Webshop when prompted.
3. The website obtains a single-use ticket and connects automatically. Do not enter or share a Minecraft password.

The plugin's Jetty port is not a player-facing website and should remain private behind the reverse proxy.
